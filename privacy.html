Privacy Policy

hermes — Personal Google Workspace Integration · Last updated: 2026-10-04

1. Overview

hermes ("the App") is a private, single-user application operated solely by its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its developer/owner.

2. Data We Access

With the owner's explicit OAuth consent, the App may access the owner's own Google account data:

  • Gmail: read, send, and modify messages
  • Google Calendar: read and manage calendar events
  • Google Drive: read, upload, and organize files
  • Google Contacts: read contacts

3. How Data Is Used

Accessed data is used exclusively to perform actions requested by the owner on their own device (e.g. showing upcoming events, searching email, managing Drive files). Data is processed locally on the owner's machine.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or transmit the owner's Google data to any third party. No data is sent to external analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

5. Data Storage

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's private machine (~/.hermes/google_token.json) and are used only to authenticate requests to Google on the owner's behalf. Tokens can be revoked at any time by the owner.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
