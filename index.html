hermes

Personal Google Workspace integration

About

hermes is a private, single-user application that connects a personal Hermes Agent instance to the owner's own Google account. It is used exclusively by its developer/owner to read and manage their own Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive on their personal machine. It is not distributed to or used by any third party.

Purpose

  • Read and send email from the owner's own Gmail account
  • View and manage the owner's own calendar events
  • Search, upload, and organize files in the owner's own Google Drive
